Our focus

From molecular understanding to predictive design

The Molecular Computation and Design Lab develops computational approaches that combine machine learning, statistical mechanics, molecular simulation, and quantum-mechanical calculations to uncover how molecular structure and dynamics determine function. We use these insights to predict, discover, and design molecules and materials for drug discovery, energy storage, and advanced computing.
