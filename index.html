Our focus

Understanding molecular processes and designing new materials and molecules.

The Molecular Computation and Design Lab develops and applies machine learning, statistical mechanics, and quantum calculations to reveal how molecular structure, interactions, and dynamics govern function. By connecting fundamental insight with predictive design, we aim to create new molecules and materials for drug discovery, energy storage, and advanced computing.
